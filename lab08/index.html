Jacob Ducret's Biography

Professional Summary

During the COVID-19 pandemic, Jacob Ducret realized the significance and severity mental health issues possessed. Seeing his friends and loved ones suffer lit a fire under him, igniting a passion and future career in mental health care. Currently, he is a sophmore student at Syracuse University studying Psychology, with an interest in pediatric psychiatry and entrepreneurship. For the longest time, Jacob was sure he wanted to become a psychiatrist. Always being the one his friends went to when going through difficult times, Jacob knew he had an innate skill to help people through their troubles, and he genuinely enjoyed it too. He believed that skill would translate into being a psychiatrist, where he would be able to help more than just his friends, and make a lasting impact on the lives of many. Now, however, with his growing hobby of reading financial literacy books, he wonders if he could mix psychology and entrepreneurship, to create a product or service that would make therapy and mental health care more accessible to the world, impacting more lives than being a psychiatrist ever could.

Personal Info

Aside from academics, Jacob enjoys TV shows and food like any other teenager. His favorite shows include Saved by the Bell, The Boondocks, and Spongebob Squarepants. As for food, his favorite is pastelitos, also the first food he ever successfully cooked on his own. Other hobbies of his include playing basketball, reading books, playing Clash Royale and watching Youtube.

Family Info

Jacob Ducret is a middle child with three other brothers. His older having graduated from Fordham University with a bachelors, and his younger brothers still in high school and elementary school. His father is a Vice President at BHI, an Israeli Bank, meanwhile his mother is a medical technician assistance at Mount Sinai. Some common interests among his family revolve around culture and sports. His family, whether it be his mother's Dominician side or his father's Puerto Rican side, are proud to be Hispanic, and love the opportunity to embrace and show off their culture. Whether it's the music, the parties, or the food, there is sure to be some aspect of Hispanic culture in everything his family does. That passion remains the same when it comes to sports, with his family being majority New York Yankee and New York Knicks fans, when New York wins, it's a happy day in the Ducret household.
