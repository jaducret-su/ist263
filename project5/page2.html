These patterns exist on a spectrum. Everyone will experience at least one of these at some point in their life. Having one does not mean there is something wrong with you.

MindMirror is a tool to help you understand your mind and how it works. It is not a replacement for therapy, but it can be a helpful supplement to therapy. It can also be a helpful tool for people who are not ready to seek therapy, but want to understand themselves better.
