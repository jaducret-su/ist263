Explanations

Overthinking

What it is: Overthinking is not a lack of control. It is your brain attempting to prevent future harm by replaying information.

Why it Makes Sense: Your brain is designed to reduce uncertainty. Replaying situations feels productive because repetition signals preparation and safety.

Avoidance

What it is: Avoidance is not laziness. It is your nervous system trying to protect you from discomfort.

Why it Makes Sense: Avoiding something reduces anxiety in the short term, teaching your brain that avoidance equals safety.

People Pleasing

What it is: People-pleasing is a survival strategy, not a personality flaw.

Why it Makes Sense: Maintaining harmony can feel necessary when approval, safety, or stability depended on others’ reactions.

Emotional Numbness

What it is: Emotional numbness is a protective shutdown, not a lack of emotion.

Why it Makes Sense: When emotions feel overwhelming or unsafe, the nervous system may dampen them to maintain functioning.
